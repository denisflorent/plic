Share media and files

Attach files

Click the attachment button in the compose bar or use the file picker. You can stage multiple files before sending.

Drag and drop

Drag files from Finder onto the compose bar or timeline. A drop target overlay appears to confirm the drop zone.

Paste images

Paste images from the clipboard directly into the compose bar with ⌘V.

Captions

Add optional caption text to each attachment before sending.

Media previews

Images and videos display inline in the timeline. Audio files show a player control. Animated GIFs can be set to animate:

  • Always
  • On hover
  • Never

Open attachments

Attachments you receive are decrypted in memory, and Plic never writes them to disk on its own. It opens them itself:

  • Images and videos — click to open them in a viewer window. Press Esc to close it.
  • PDF documents — double-click to open them in the same viewer.
  • Audio and voice messages — click the play button, or double-click the message, to play them in place.
  • Other files — double-click to save them, then open the saved copy in another app.

To keep a copy of any attachment, hover over it and click the download button. The file is saved decrypted, where you choose.

Control media visibility

In Settings › Behavior, you can control whether media previews load automatically, and whether they appear in all rooms or only private rooms.

Tip: You can also share files from other apps using the system Share menu. Shared files appear staged in Plic's compose bar for review before sending.
